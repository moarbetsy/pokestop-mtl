Shipping Policy

Last updated: February 2025

At PokéStop MTL we ship with care so your collectibles arrive safely. Please review the following shipping information.

Shipping Areas

We currently ship within Canada. Quebec and Ontario orders are our most common; we also ship to all other Canadian provinces and territories. International shipping may be offered in the future.

Rates & Free Shipping

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight. Free shipping on orders over $100 (before tax) within Canada. Otherwise, standard rates apply.

Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days (excluding holidays). You will receive an email confirmation when your order is placed and when it ships.

Delivery Time

Typical delivery within Canada is 3–7 business days after shipment, depending on your location. Remote areas may take longer. Delivery times are estimates and not guaranteed.

Tracking & Insurance

All shipments are tracked. You will receive a tracking number by email once your order has shipped. High-value orders may be insured; we recommend ensuring adequate coverage for valuable cards.

Packaging

Cards and sealed products are packed with protective materials to minimize damage in transit. Graded cards are shipped in secure holders and sturdy boxes.

Delays & Lost Packages

Delays can occur due to weather, customs (if applicable), or carrier issues. If your package is significantly delayed or lost, contact us at info@pokestopmtl.com with your order number and we will assist you.

Local Pickup

Montreal-area customers may have the option to pick up orders in person. Select "Local pickup" at checkout if available. We will confirm pickup location and hours by email.
